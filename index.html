Meet the Founder and Director

Mannat Sharma is a Grade 12 student at Delhi Public School Alwar, Rajasthan. She is interested in social issues, education, public health, and research, and enjoys exploring how different fields can be connected to real-world problems. Alongside her academics, she has been involved in student leadership, community initiatives, research projects, and educational activities.
