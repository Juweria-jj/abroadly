About
Abroadly

Built by students, for students looking beyond borders

Abroadly is a student-led community that curates real international opportunities — research, scholarships, internships, projects — and connects you with mentors who have done it.


We were tired of scattered links and paywalled advice. So we built a warm, honest space: no spam, no fake promises, just guidance that actually helps.

What we do

1:1 mentorship for passion projects
Project Lab with templates
Journal with real student stories
Community board to find teammates

Why it matters

First-gen, international, small-town — if you are curious, you belong.

Our motto — learn together, lead your own project, rise with community.
